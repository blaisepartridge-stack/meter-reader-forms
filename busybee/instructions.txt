BUSYBEE - HOW TO TRY IT
Test version 1 (2 November 2026)

WHAT IS BUSYBEE?
BusyBee helps you remember things. You make "hives" (groups) with
your own names, like "School pick-up" or "Animal tablets".
Each hive has its own reminders and its own sound settings.


OPEN IT
Option 1: Scan QR code 1 with your phone camera.
          Tap the link that pops up.

Option 2: Tap this link:
https://blaisepartridge-stack.github.io/meter-reader-forms/busybee/


READ THIS FIRST
- Reminders only ring while BusyBee is open on your screen.
  Keep the phone unlocked and the screen on at reminder time.
- Turn the sound up.
- iPhone: if you hear nothing, check the switch on the side of
  the phone is not on silent.
- Vibrate does not work on iPhone. Sound still works.


ON AN APPLE iPHONE OR iPAD
1. Open the link in SAFARI. (Safari is best for the microphone.)
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4. Tap "Add". BusyBee now has its own icon on your home screen.
5. The first time you tap the microphone, tap "Allow".


ON AN ANDROID PHONE
1. Open the link in CHROME.
2. Tap the three dots (top right corner).
3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
4. Tap "Install" or "Add".
5. The first time you tap the microphone, tap "Allow".


HOW TO USE IT

Make a hive
- Tap "+ New hive". The settings open.
- Type a name, or tap the microphone and say it.
- Tap Save.

Change a hive
- Tap the gear (⚙️) on the hive, or tap "⚙️ Hive settings".
- You can change the name, the alert type, the sound and the volume.
- Tap Delete hive to remove a hive and its reminders.

Add a reminder
- Tap the hive you want.
- Tap "✨ Add reminder".
- Type the reminder, or tap the microphone and say it.
- Pick the time and how it repeats.
- Pick Alarm, Silent or Vibrate.
- Pick a sound and move the volume slider.
- Tap Save.

Reminder buttons in the list
- ▶ = test it now
- ✏️ = change it
- 🗑️ = delete it

When a reminder goes off
- 💤 5 min or 💤 10 min = remind me again later
- ✔ Keep for next time = close it, keep it for its next time
- ✏️ Edit = fix a mistake (for example, from voice)
- 🗑️ Delete = remove it
- An Alarm keeps ringing until you tap a button.
  It stops by itself after 2 minutes.

Use your own sound
- In Hive settings, or in a reminder, tap "🎵 Add my own sound".
- Pick a sound or music file from your phone.
- The file must be under 8 MB.
- Your sound is saved on this phone only.

Talk to BusyBee
- Tap the big microphone at the top to say a reminder.
- It fills in the reminder form. Check it, then tap Save.
- Voice can get words wrong. Fix them with ✏️ Edit.


NOT IN THIS VERSION YET
- Smartwatch reminders
- Reminders that ring with the phone locked
- Ask BusyBee (finding things you forgot)
- Notes, Prompts, Memories and sections
- Fireworks alert style
- Repeats other than every day or chosen days
  (no fortnightly, monthly or yearly yet)
