BUSYBEE - HOW TO TRY IT
Test version 2 (2 November 2026)


OPEN IT
Option 1: Scan QR code 1 with your phone camera. Tap the link.

Option 2: Tap this link:
https://blaisepartridge-stack.github.io/meter-reader-forms/busybee/


READ THIS FIRST
- Reminders only ring while BusyBee is open on your screen.
  Keep the phone unlocked and the screen on at reminder time.
- Turn the sound up.
- iPhone: if you hear nothing, check the switch on the side of
  the phone is not on silent.
- Vibrate does not work on iPhone. Sound still works.


INSTALL ON AN APPLE iPHONE OR iPAD
1. Open the link in SAFARI. (Safari is best for the microphone.)
2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4. Tap "Add". BusyBee now has its own icon on your home screen.
5. The first time you tap the microphone, tap "Allow".


INSTALL ON AN ANDROID PHONE
1. Open the link in CHROME.
2. Tap the three dots (top right corner).
3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
4. Tap "Install" or "Add".
5. The first time you tap the microphone, tap "Allow".


THE BIG BUTTON AT THE BOTTOM: BRAIN DUMP
Tap "🧠 Brain Dump · Talk to me" at the bottom of the screen.
This is your scrapbook for ideas. Use it for anything, half-finished
is fine.
- Talk: tap the 🎤 and speak. Your words appear in the box.
- Type: just type in the box.
- Save: tap "💾 Save". BusyBee splits your words into separate
  items at commas and the word "and". Turn off "Split into separate
  items" to keep everything as one item.
- Search: type a word in the search box, like "milk" or "Jack".
  Only matching items show.
- Categories: the buttons show Idea, Shopping, Bill, Call or
  Reminder. BusyBee guesses the category from your words. You can
  change it with the drop-down on each item.
- Make a reminder: tap ⏰ on an item. The reminder form opens with
  your words filled in. Pick a hive, a time and an alert, then Save.
- Delete: tap 🗑️.


HIVES
- Make a hive: tap "+ New hive". Type a name, or tap 🎤 and say it.
  Tap Save.
- Starter hives: tap "🧩 Starter hives" to add School pick-up,
  Animal tablets, Canteen roster, Bins and Rent. Rename any of them.
- Change a hive: tap the gear (⚙️) on it, or "⚙️ Hive settings".
  You can change the name, alert, sound and volume. "Delete hive"
  removes it and its reminders.


REMINDERS
- Add one: tap "✨ Add reminder", or tap the big 🎤 at the top and
  say it. Pick a hive, a time, how it repeats and an alert.
- Alerts:
    🔔 Alarm = rings with a sound until you tap a button
    🔕 Silent = pop-up only
    📳 Vibrate = buzzes and plays your sound once
    ✨ Fireworks = colours across the screen, no sound
- Buttons on each reminder:
    ✔ = done (it turns grey and crossed out, and a little bee
        celebrates)
    ▶ = test it now
    ✏️ = change it
    🗑️ = delete it
- "Next up" box: shows your next reminder with a ring. The ring is
  full when there are 2 hours or more to go, and empty when it is
  time.


WHEN A REMINDER GOES OFF
- ✔ Done = finished
- 💤 5 min or 💤 10 min = remind me again later
- ↪ Keep for next time = close it, keep it for its next time
- ✏️ Edit = fix a mistake (for example, from voice)
- 🗑️ Delete = remove it
- Alarms stop by themselves after 2 minutes.
- The pop-up stays on screen until you tap a button.


YOUR OWN SOUNDS
- In Hive settings, or in a reminder, tap "🎵 Add my own sound".
- Pick a sound or music file from your phone (under 8 MB).
- It is saved on this phone only.


NOT IN THIS VERSION YET
- Reminders that ring with the phone locked
- Smartwatch reminders
- Siri or Google Assistant commands
- Sharing hives with other people
- AI features: "Where's my...?" search and "I'm stuck" steps
  (these cost money, so they wait for approval)
- Notes, Prompts, Memories and other sections
- Repeats other than every day or chosen days
- Fireworks and alarms only work while the page is open
